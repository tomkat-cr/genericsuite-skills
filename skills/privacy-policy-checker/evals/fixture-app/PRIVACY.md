# MindJournal Privacy Policy

_Last updated: March 2, 2021_

Lumaworks Ltd. ("we", "us") builds MindJournal, a journaling app. This policy
explains what we collect and how we use it.

## Information we collect

- **Account information.** When you sign up we collect your email address, your
  display name and your date of birth.
- **Journal entries.** The text of the entries you write is stored in your
  account so you can read it back on any device.
- **Photos.** If you attach a photo to an entry, that photo is stored with the
  entry.
- **Device and diagnostic data.** We collect app version, device model, OS
  version and crash reports so we can fix problems.
- **Usage analytics.** We record which screens you open and which features you
  use, so we can understand how MindJournal is used.

We do not collect your location.

## How we use your information

We use your information to provide the journaling service, to keep your entries
synchronised across your devices, to send you the reminder notifications you
have enabled, and to diagnose crashes.

## Sharing

**We never share, sell or rent your personal information to third parties.**
Your journal entries are private to you.

## Data retention

We keep your entries for as long as your account is active. If you delete your
account, your entries are permanently removed from our servers within 30 days.

## Security

We use industry-standard encryption in transit and at rest.

## Children

MindJournal is not intended for children under 13 and we do not knowingly
collect information from them.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced
in the app.
